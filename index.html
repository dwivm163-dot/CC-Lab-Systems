Saved for Later

01 / ABOUT

A data self-portrait

What do my saved pins say about who I want to become?

Saved for Later is an interactive collage made from my exported Pinterest data. It looks at the gap between the version of myself I collect online and the life I actually live.

Each collage begins with expectation. Clickable pins will flip in place to reveal moments from reality, gradually turning the page into a mix of both.
